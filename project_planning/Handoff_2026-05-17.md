## 1. Project in one paragraph

Robust multimodal hateful-meme detection (EE-559 EPFL course project).
OpenCLIP ViT-B/32 (`laion2b_s34b_b79k`) image + text encoders, frozen,
feeding a fusion head over `[t, v, |t−v|, t⊙v] ∈ ℝ^2048`. Image
normalisation is *inside* the model (takes [0,1] pixels) so the same
forward graph powers training, consistency-loss KL, and white-box image
attacks. Five training recipes exist on the `perturbations` branch:
`stage1` (clean), `robust-augonly`, `robust-kl`, `robust-kldrop`,
`robust-kllowmed`. Eval surface is 57 single-perturbation cells
(8 text × 3 + 11 image × 3, where one of the 11 is `typographic`)
plus 12 composite cells (4 types × 3 severities), plus white-box
FGSM/PGD-10 at ε ∈ {1,2,4,8}/255, plus a modality-ablation forward.

## 4. Phase 5c findings (the new part)

Three follow-up extensions on Phase 5b, all done.

**5c-1 (free, no cluster cost) — in-pool/OOD + per-severity tables.**

- Δ(OOD−in) image is *negative* for every robust recipe → augmentation
  gains transfer to held-out image attacks the model never saw.
- Monotone low ≤ medium ≤ high ΔAUROC for every (recipe, family) row —
  sanity gate passed.
- At medium severity (internet-realistic): `kldrop` cuts text-family
  ΔAUROC by 46 % vs clean (0.063 → 0.034).
- Text-side OOD column has limited signal (held-out text attacks are
  intrinsically weak); image-side OOD is the meaningful test.

**5c-2 (~1.5 A100-h) — composite attacks.**

12 new perturbed-eval cells per ckpt: `composite_{2text, 2image,
text_image, 2text_2image}` × {low, medium, high}. Per-sample components
drawn deterministically from `sha256(cell_seed | sample_id)`, sampled
*without replacement* per modality from the full eval pool (composite
is itself an OOD test).

- **Composite attacks escalate the threat past the single-cell worst.**
  Clean-ckpt `composite_2text_2image` at high severity hits ΔAUROC
  0.145, vs the prior single-cell worst of 0.115. The "worst-cell"
  headline from § 5/5b is a single-attack ceiling, not the
  worst-case-over-realistic-threats ceiling.
- **`kldrop` is the strict Pareto winner on every composite cell.**
  Best at every (composite-type × severity), at every severity. Cuts
  medium-severity `composite_2text` by 43 % vs clean (0.090 → 0.051);
  `composite_text_image` by 51 % (0.050 → 0.024);
  `composite_2text_2image` by 41 % (0.080 → 0.047). The
  modality-dropout intervention pays off most when the attack hits
  both modalities at once.
- Monotonicity (low ≤ med ≤ high) holds for every (recipe × composite
  type) row.

**5c-3 (~2 A100-h) — severity-restricted training (`kl_lowmed`).**

Config: `configs/stage1_robust_kl_lowmed.yaml`. Identical to
`stage1_robust_kl.yaml` except `severity_weights_text: [1,2,0]` and
`severity_weights_image: [1,1,0]` — high severity weight is 0,
excluding it from the augmenter via `random.choices`.

- **Clean negative result**: `kl_lowmed` is Pareto-dominated by `kl`
  on every metric. Clean AUROC tied within σ (0.7347 vs 0.7367) but
  text ΔAUROC at medium +0.002 (worse), at high +0.003, image-only
  AUROC −0.009, naturalistic-robust count 107.3 vs 111.7.
- **Interpretation**: high-severity training augmentation is
  load-bearing. Removing it produces a strictly worse model even at
  medium severity. The original Phase-5 severity weights `[1,2,2]` /
  `[1,1,1]` were *not* over-tuned toward high.
- Publishable as a methodology check, not as a competing recipe.

## 7. Known cluster facts you should not re-discover

- `submit-cmd <job> -- <tokens>` is the canonical way to launch
  non-canned scripts on Run:AI. Job names must be unique — for
  overwrites, `job-delete <job>` first.
- `pull-results` is a plain rsync of
  `epfl-jumphost:.../experiments/` → local `cluster-results/`. Can
  take a few minutes; the underlying rsync is the actual long-running
  process.
- `runai-list` is the only built-in status command; for individual job
  details use `cluster.sh ssh runai describe job <name>`.
- GPU-node contention has been observed: training jobs sometimes land
  on a busy node and take 1h+ instead of ~12 min. Not a code issue,
  just wait it out.
- The `whitebox-robust-kllowmed-seed*` job directories on the cluster
  exist *before* the eval runs because the entrypoint creates the
  output dir on submit; only the `whitebox_eval.json` shows up at the
  end. Don't be confused by an empty local dir mid-run.
